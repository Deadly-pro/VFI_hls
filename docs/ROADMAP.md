# Roadmap

Progress log. The minimum useful artifact — a verified, synthesized,
weight-reloadable INT8 layer — is done; the remaining work is scaling the
datapath and locking in the new synthesis flow.

## Done

- **Toolchain**: Icarus Verilog 13.0, Yosys, cocotb, numpy/matplotlib, and the
  Sky130 PDK installed and working end to end.
- **Primitives**: `mac_int8`, `weight_mem` (reloadable), `line_buffer`,
  `requantize` — all verified bit-exact.
- **First full layer**: `dw_conv3x3` + `pw_conv1x1` composed into
  `ds_conv_layer`, then `ds_conv_layer_integrated` with `weight_mem` wired in —
  weights reload at runtime over `wl_we/wl_addr/wl_data` with no resynthesis.
- **Encoder**: `encoder_slice` (E1 -> E2, stride-2), verified end to end.
- **Streaming/parallel upgrades**: `line_buffer_stream` (1 pixel in / 1 window
  out) and `pw_conv1x1_parallel` (PARALLEL_CO-way MAC array).
- **Warp + blend**: `warp_unit` (bilinear grid_sample, Q8.8), `blend_unit`,
  and `vfi_synth` top-level, all verified bit-exact against `golden/`.
- **Demo**: `tools/vfi_demo.py` turns two frames into an interpolated one
  through the RTL INT8 path, PSNR 54.7 dB vs the FP16 ONNX reference.
- **Verification gate**: all 14 cocotb suites pass via `./run_all_tests.sh`.

## Synthesis

Two flows are in the repo.

**Vivado 2026.1 on Artix-7 XC7A100T** (`syn/vivado/`) — the current flow,
100 MHz target. Per-module utilization/WNS reports and a summarized table are
generated by `run_vivado.sh` + `summarize_vivado.py`. Status:

- All 13 modules synthesized (utilization/WNS/Fmax table in
  `reports/VFI_DL_Report.md` and `reports/plots/vivado_results.png`).
- The 6 CNN modules with unpacked array ports (`dw_conv3x3`, `pw_conv1x1`,
  `pw_conv1x1_parallel`, `ds_conv_layer`, `ds_conv_layer_integrated`,
  `encoder_slice`) were rejected by Vivado's Verilog parser — fixed by reading
  the RTL as SystemVerilog (`read_verilog -sv`). All except `encoder_slice`
  synthesize; the CNN layers close at 55–73 MHz on this part.
- `encoder_slice` (serial C_OUT=96 PW FSM) **does not finish synthesis** in
  reasonable time — the serial-PW datapath blows up Vivado's logic
  optimization. `pw_conv1x1_parallel` is the documented fix; the integrated
  path is verified in simulation and the parallel-PW layer itself synthesizes.
- `warp_unit` / `vfi_synth` close at ~34 MHz post-synthesis (~31 MHz
  post-route), the combinational four-port gather from the frame buffer is the
  critical path. Pipelining the frame-buffer read is the obvious next lever.
- `vfi_synth` post-route: 12,287 LUT / 12,478 FF / 24 DSP, 0.318 W total
  (0.226 W dynamic) at the 100 MHz constraint.

## Gotchas (read before touching the RTL)

These cost real debugging time and are worth keeping on file:

- **iverilog rejects block-scoped `integer x = expr` initializers** — the X
  value propagates into registers (hit in 3 suites). Declare first, assign
  inside the block.
- **Vivado's Verilog parser rejects unpacked array ports in `.v` files**
  (`port must not be declared to be an array`). Read the RTL as SystemVerilog
  (`read_verilog -sv`), or use the flat-port wrappers in `rtl/synth/`.
- **Weight-address collisions**: the DW and PW stages share the weight-load bus;
  `encoder_slice`'s E1/E2 stages collide without a `WL_BASE` offset per stage.
- **State encodings must not collide**: `S_OUTPUT=2'd4` truncated to 0 and hit
  `S_IDLE`.
- **Synchronous reset** for synthesis compatibility (asynchronous resets are
  fine functionally but complicate the flow); `line_buffer_stream` needed the
  sync-reset rewrite.
